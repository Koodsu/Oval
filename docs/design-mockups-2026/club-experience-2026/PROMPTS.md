# Club Experience ImageGen Prompt Set

## Shared master prompt

> Create an extraordinarily polished, shippable native iOS product-design contact sheet for Oval, a college community app. Match the existing Oval design boards closely: premium black iPhone 15 Pro frames, authentic 9:41 status bars, warm white editorial presentation, crisp near-black typography, Oval scarlet `#E10600`, warm off-white solid surfaces, subtle neutral borders, and restrained blur only on navigation chrome. Use scarlet sparingly for active controls and high-value actions. Keep 44-point tap targets, readable type, strong hierarchy, and WCAG-conscious contrast. Center a warm, diverse college community through candid campus photography and tasteful editorial illustration. Use no official university logos, unrelated brands, watermarks, gibberish, Android UI, excessive gradients, neon, or glass everywhere.

All light boards use the same fictional `Photography Club` and recurring people so the ten boards read as one coherent product system.

## 01 · Club member experience

Four screens: a content-rich member club home; a warm club-home empty state with useful next actions; About and member roles; and the native club actions sheet with share, settings, report, and leave controls.

## 02 · Club conversations

Four screens: active general chat; empty role channel with `Start the conversation`; official announcements with meeting attachments; and a reassuring private officer-channel state.

## 03 · Club events and attendance

Four screens: grouped events list; empty events with member and officer actions; full meeting detail with RSVP and location; and a live member check-in state.

## 04 · Club people and joining

Four screens: grouped searchable roster; no-results roster state; full application form; and under-review application status.

## 05 · Officer desk and people

Four screens: grouped officer desk; application queue; private applicant-review sheet with accept and decline actions; and member role/tag moderation.

## 06 · Officer club controls

Four screens: role creation and organization; channel creation and visibility; per-officer permission toggles; and club photo, cover, name, description, and category editing.

## 07 · Officer communications and events

Four screens: announcement composer; meeting creation; live leader attendance console with code and progress; and targeted bulk outreach with recipient preview.

## 08 · Club lifecycle and safety

Four screens: new application-cycle builder; empty open application cycle; Instagram-based discoverability verification; and careful permanent club deletion confirmation.

## 09 · Clubs after dark

Four representative dark-mode screens: club home, general chat, meeting detail, and officer desk. Use matte charcoal surfaces, graphite borders, accessible light text, rich photography, and restrained scarlet highlights without a purple or cyberpunk cast.

## 10 · Club recovery states

Four real recovery and restriction states from the current club flows: club load error, meeting not found, applications closed, and leader access required. Every state uses calm language, a purposeful illustration, and an unambiguous next action.

## Generation mode

The boards were generated with the built-in ImageGen workflow using the existing Oval images as visual references. Individual screens were losslessly cropped from the generated contact sheets for implementation handoff.
