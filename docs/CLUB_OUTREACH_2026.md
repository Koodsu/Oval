# Oval club outreach — Fall 2026

Use this for small, individually addressed batches. The main promise to a club leader
is **year-round discovery**: Oval helps students find their club when they are actively
looking for people and plans, not only during the involvement fair or the week an
Instagram post happens. Meetings, announcements, chat, and attendance support that
promise; they are not the lead.

The goal is a 15-minute setup that ends with a real officer, a discoverable club page,
and a real meeting or announcement in Oval.

## Personalization lines that are actually specific

Use one fact from the directory, club site, or recent social post. Never invent one.

- “I saw that you run weekly pickup games, which is exactly the kind of recurring
  plan Oval should make easier for members to find.”
- “Your recent [EVENT NAME] post made me think Oval could give members a simple place
  to see the next event and continue the conversation afterward.”
- “Because your membership is open and you recruit in the fall, Oval could give new
  students a low-friction way to discover the club and find the first meeting.”
- “You already coordinate several types of events, so having meetings, announcements,
  and attendance in one place seems especially useful for your officers.”
- “The involvement fair creates one big discovery moment; Oval could keep [CLUB]
  visible to interested students for the rest of the year.”

## Qualification rule

Prioritize a club when at least three are true:

- active or recently updated listing/social account;
- open or recurring membership;
- holds real meetings, practices, outings, or events;
- roughly 20–100 reachable members;
- social, recreation, hobby, creative, service, cultural, or community fit;
- a named leader or working organization email is available;
- likely to make at least one August 19–31 post or event.

Skip inactive/revoked groups, stale contacts, and organizations whose activity would
create an obvious safety or moderation risk. Verify status in the official directory.

## Batch checklist

Before sending:

- [ ] Addressed to one person or one working organization inbox
- [ ] Club name and contact name are correct
- [ ] One true personalization sentence
- [ ] Two concrete 15-minute options in Columbus time
- [ ] `theovalapp.com` link works
- [ ] Signature uses the intended sender address
- [ ] No BCC blast and no attachment

After a positive reply:

1. Confirm one 15-minute time and ask the officer to create an Oval account first.
2. Collect the exact club name, short description, category, and emoji/avatar.
3. Use `docs/onboard-a-club.md` to assign OWNER access.
4. Do not end the setup until the officer posts a real meeting or announcement.
5. Ask which GroupMe/Discord/Instagram channel will carry the August 18 announcement.
