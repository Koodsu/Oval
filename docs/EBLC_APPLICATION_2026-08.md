## Tell us about your venture

> Oval is an iOS app that helps Ohio State students turn loose intentions into real in-person plans. The core object is a "pod" — a small, time-boxed hangout tied to a specific activity (pickup frisbee, a dining hall dinner, a study session at Thompson, a lift at the RPAC). A student creates a pod with a time, a location, and a size cap; other students browse open pods on a campus feed and map, join one, coordinate in a group chat, and show up.
>
> Around that core, the app includes club discovery (public and private clubs, meetings, announcements, club chat, and officer tools for student organizations), direct messaging, a friend graph with invites and post-hangout "people you met" flows, and push notifications for reminders and check-ins.
>
> The mission is to lower the activation energy for real-world social connection on a campus of 60,000+ students. Large universities are paradoxically isolating: students are surrounded by people but lack a low-stakes way to convert "I'd like to do something tonight" into an actual plan with actual people. Existing tools are either broadcast-oriented (Instagram, GroupMe) or built for strangers dating rather than for classmates hanging out. Oval is deliberately small-group, campus-gated, and plan-shaped.
>
> Trust and safety are core to the product rather than bolted on. Every account requires a verified osu.edu email address before it can see or do anything meaningful. The app has blocking, reporting, no-show tracking, account deletion, and user data export, and carries a 17+/18+ age rating so that it is used only by adult students. Those features exist both because the product doesn't work without trust and because Apple's review process requires them.
>
> Oval is live on the App Store (version 1.1 shipped in July 2026) and is currently free to users with no advertising and no paid tier.
